fallback:fpe_domain_too_small

FF1 needs at least 10⁶ free values. Keeping the last 4 of a 9-digit SSN leaves 5 digits; a 15-digit Amex with first 6 and last 4 kept leaves 5. Those values run as det_token, still reversible. To keep format-preserving output, drop keep_suffix for SSN.

fallback:fpe_disabled

Every fpe_* method runs as det_token on a deployment where FF1 is off (DPS_FPE_ENABLED=false until the licence review signs it off). Switch the FF1 engine to Off above to see that plan.
